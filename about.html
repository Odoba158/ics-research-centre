About ICS Research Centre

Founded in Accra, Ghana, ICS Research Centre is an independent research institution delivering gold-standard polling, market analytics, and econometric impact evaluations.

Our Heritage

A Decade of Independent Applied Research

Over the past decade, ICS Research Centre has built an unmatched track record of empirical evaluations for public ministries, bilateral development partners, multilateral financial institutions, and corporate enterprises. We operate as an autonomous, scientifically non-partisan research organization headquartered in Accra, Ghana.

Too often in developing economies, key policy and resource allocation choices are made without verified microdata, or reliance is placed on outdated aggregate figures that mask deep subnational disparities. ICS Research Centre was founded specifically to close that evidentiary gap: deploying trained local enumerator networks, cutting-edge CAPI mobile data pipelines, and reproducible econometric methods to generate findings that withstand international due diligence.

From evaluating primary health post staffing in the Savannah Ecological Zone to measuring food price transmission across 42 commercial markets and auditing municipal fiscal transfers, our work directly informs national legislation, sectoral reforms, and institutional donor financing.

Research Ethics & Human Subjects Review Board

All polling and survey research involving human participants is governed by our Institutional Review Board (IRB) ethical charter.

1. Informed Voluntary Consent

Participation in all Centre field surveys is strictly voluntary. Survey instruments require administered verbal or signed written informed consent translated into the respondent's primary mother tongue. Participants may terminate or skip questions at any juncture.

2. Vulnerable Population Safeguards

Surveys touching upon juvenile health, maternal health, informal mineral extraction enclaves, or displaced communities require secondary ethical clearance. Where children are assessed, dual consent is collected from legal guardians alongside verbal assent.

3. Analytical Independence

The Centre accepts commissioned research under terms ensuring analytical independence. Commercial clients or sponsoring ministries hold no right of veto over empirical findings, econometric specifications, or statistical conclusions derived from verified data.

Capabilities & Services

Research Programmes & Corporate Services

Our programmes are engineered to deliver high-precision evidence for political leaders, executive boards, multilateral development banks, and government ministries across Ghana and the West African subregion.

SERVICE 01

Public Opinion Polling & Governance Analytics

We deploy continuous national sentiment tracking, political opinion polling, and public policy satisfaction surveys. Using Multilevel Regression with Post-stratification (MRP), we generate robust constituency-level predictions from sample data across all 275 parliamentary constituencies in Ghana.

Core Capabilities

  • Nationwide presidential and parliamentary opinion polling models.
  • Citizen satisfaction surveys regarding public services (water, electricity, roads, sanitation).
  • Subnational fiscal transfer tracking (District Assemblies Common Fund - DACF).
  • Perception audits of institutional trust, corruption indices, and judicial transparency.
Citable Research

Evaluating Subnational Fiscal Transfers and District Service Delivery in Ghana (2018–2025)

Lead Author: Daniel Oppong • DOI: 10.5281/ics.2026.04

Commission a Polling Study
SERVICE 02

Corporate Market Research & Inflation Intelligence

In fast-shifting retail environments, enterprise leaders need reliable primary intelligence. We operate a continuous commodity price tracking network across 42 commercial markets and conduct consumer sentiment, brand equity, and customer churn studies.

Core Capabilities

  • High-frequency food basket price tracking and rural-urban transport markups.
  • Consumer goods brand health, market share estimation, and retail shelf audit.
  • Informal enterprise income dynamics, credit access, and mobile money taxation effects.
  • Fintech adoption and micro-savings patterns among peri-urban merchants.
Market Series

Food Price Inflation Pass-Through: Micro-Evidence from 42 Market Centres

Lead Author: Aaron Donkor • DOI: 10.5281/ics.2026.02

Request Market Research Proposal
SERVICE 03

Applied Statistical Modelling & Econometric Causal Inference

Our scientific econometric core ensures every evaluation produces mathematically sound causal conclusions rather than mere correlational observations.

Core Capabilities

  • Difference-in-Differences (DiD), Instrumental Variables (IV), and Synthetic Controls.
  • Small-Area Estimation (SAE) combining census records with sample survey data.
  • Monte Carlo power calculations and clustered sampling optimization for field RCTs.
  • Oster bounds for unobservable selection and wild cluster bootstrapping.
Technical Report

Predictive Small-Area Estimation for Health Facility Resource Allocation

Lead Author: Dr. Kwame Mensah • DOI: 10.5281/ics.2025.11

Inspect Statistical Standards
SERVICE 04

Health Systems & Development Metrics

Assessing public health infrastructure delivery, maternal-child health metrics, and healthcare financing equity across underserved districts.

Core Capabilities

  • Community Health Planning and Services (CHPS) compound staffing retention studies.
  • Pharmaceutical supply-chain stockout monitoring and cold-chain integrity audits.
  • Maternal healthcare access and emergency obstetric referral transit bottlenecks.
  • National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS) claims verification and financial equity.
Policy Brief

CHPS Compound Retention Dynamics: Administrative Survey Evidence

Lead Author: Dr. Abena Boateng • DOI: 10.5281/ics.2025.08

Commission Health Study
SERVICE 05

CAPI Survey Infrastructure & Capacity Training

We deploy Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing (CAPI) survey platforms, automated daily anomaly detection algorithms, and deliver executive econometric workshops for public institutions.

Core Capabilities

  • CAPI survey platform architecture (SurveyCTO / ODK) with audio audits and GPS bounding.
  • Statistical disclosure control (SDC) and k-anonymity microdata curation.
  • Executive training in Stata, R, spatial analytics, and survey sampling.
  • Open data repository development compliant with Ghana Act 843.

Daniel Oppong

Chief Executive Officer
Office of the Directorate • Accra, Ghana

Daniel Oppong is the Chief Executive Officer of ICS Research Centre. Over the past decade, Daniel has directed commissioned public policy evaluations, subnational fiscal tracking programmes, and economic impact analyses for government ministries, multilateral agencies, and international development partners. His research specializes in public expenditure efficiency, inter-governmental fiscal relations (notably District Assemblies Common Fund dynamics), and applied taxation incidence in developing economies.

Core Technical Domains
  • Applied Econometrics
  • Fiscal Decentralization
  • Public Expenditure Reviews (PETS)
  • Institutional Strategy

Aaron Donkor serves as Deputy Chief Executive Officer, overseeing research operations, statistical methodology, and quality assurance frameworks across all Centre programmes.

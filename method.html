Audit-Grade Process

Our Research Method & Sampling Protocols

How do partners know our findings can be trusted? Every commissioned study and public opinion poll at ICS Research Centre proceeds through an auditable, four-stage protocol engineered to withstand stringent audit and academic peer review.

01
Stage One

Scope, Sampling Design & Ethical Clearances

We eliminate ambiguity by registering formal Pre-Analysis Plans (PAPs) before field enumerators deploy. This phase codifies sampling frames, statistical power calculations, questionnaire scripting, and institutional IRB certifications.

Deliverables in Stage 1

  • Theoretical Framework: Formulating theories of change and explicit empirical hypotheses.
  • Clustered Sampling & Power Simulations: Monte Carlo power calculations ensuring minimum detectable effect sizes (MDE) across subnational districts.
  • Ethical Protocol & IRB Certification: Full compliance with human subjects protections under Ghanaian law and international covenants.
02
Stage Two

CAPI Field Data Collection & Quality Verification

Primary fieldwork is executed across Ghana’s 16 administrative regions using encrypted mobile CAPI (Computer-Assisted Personal Interviewing) tablets. Surveys are audited by real-time cloud anomaly detection algorithms.

Field Quality Controls

  • Nightly Anomaly Scripts: Automated algorithms flag duration outliers, suspicious question-skip clustering, or numeric digit preferences.
  • Independent 10% Back-Checks: Senior audit supervisors re-visit a random 10% sub-sample of respondents to independently verify factual responses.
  • GPS Bounding & Audio Snips: Coordinate tracking within approved sampling polygons and encrypted audio audits of question pacing.
  • Mother-Tongue Interviewers: Field staff fluent in Akan (Twi/Fante), Ga, Ewe, Dagbani, and Hausa.
03
Stage Three

Econometric Analysis & Blind Internal Replication

Raw microdata is cleaned and modeled in reproducible pipelines (Stata, R, Python). Every headline estimate undergoes blinded internal replication where a separate econometrician replicates all models from scratch.

Replication & Review Standards

  • Scripted Reproducibility: End-to-end versioned code pipelines with fixed random seed numbers for public replicability.
  • Sensitivity Bounds: Oster bounds, Lee bounds for non-random survey attrition, and wild cluster bootstrap checks.
  • Methodology Review Board: Chaired by Deputy CEO Aaron Donkor, evaluating mathematical specifications before drafting.
04
Stage Four

Publication, Open Data & Executive Briefings

We translate technical models into decision-ready executive policy briefs, citable public working papers, and interactive open datasets.

Final Handover Deliverables

  • Executive Briefings: High-impact summaries prepared for ministers, boards, and development directors.
  • Persistent Citable DOIs: Archived records on Zenodo / OSF ensuring URLs never break.
  • Anonymized Microdata Packages: Codebooks and data dictionaries released under CC-BY 4.0 licenses.

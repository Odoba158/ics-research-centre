Scientific Integrity

The Four-Stage ICS Research Process

To ensure findings withstand rigorous ministerial review, donor audits, and academic scrutiny, every engagement follows our standardized, auditable framework.

01

Scope & Design

Theory of change formulation, statistical power calculations, stratified sampling designs, and formal IRB ethical clearances.

  • Pre-analysis plan registration
  • Stakeholder theory-of-change
  • Ethical review & data protocols
02

Data Collection

Deployment of audited CAPI mobile survey instruments, dual-entry field verification, and continuous geo-tagged audit trails.

  • High-frequency anomaly checks
  • Supervisor back-checks (10%)
  • Local language enumerator teams
03

Analysis & Review

Econometric causal estimation, sensitivity bounds, blinded internal replication, and peer review by senior domain economists.

  • Fully reproducible scripts
  • Robustness & placebo tests
  • Internal methodology committee
04

Publication & Handover

Translating statistical findings into executive policy briefs, technical documentation, open datasets, and ministerial debriefings.

  • Actionable executive summaries
  • Public DOI repository hosting
  • Capacity transfer workshops

Evaluating Subnational Fiscal Transfers and District Service Delivery in Ghana (2018–2025)

Abstract: Decentralized fiscal transfers represent the primary financing vehicle for subnational capital expenditure across Ghana’s 261 Metropolitan, Municipal, and District Assemblies (MMDAs). Utilizing a newly harmonized panel of District Assemblies Common Fund (DACF) disbursement schedules and local infrastructure audits, this paper evaluates the empirical impact of transfer disbursement volatility on primary health facility completion rates. We find that a 90-day delay in quarterly tranche disbursement increases contractor demobilization probability by 31.4% and raises average project completion cost overruns by 14.8%.

Tags: Fiscal Decentralization • DACF • Municipal Governance • Public Expenditure • Ghana

DOI: 10.5281/ics.2026.02

Food Price Inflation Pass-Through: Micro-Evidence from 42 Market Centres Across Southern and Northern Ghana

Abstract: This policy brief reports findings from our high-frequency food basket price tracker across 42 urban and rural market centres between January 2025 and May 2026. While headline national inflation exhibited moderation in early 2026, staple food basket prices (maize, local rice, cooking oil, tomatoes) demonstrated stubborn spatial dispersion. Intermediary transport markups between northern producing hubs (Tamale, Techiman) and southern coastal consumer hubs (Accra, Sekondi-Takoradi) accounted for 43% of total retail price divergence.

Tags: Food Security • Spatial Inflation • Market Intermediaries • Rural Transport
Aaron Donkor (Lead)
Faustina Osei-Tutu
Daniel Oppong

Predictive Small-Area Estimation for Health Facility Resource Allocation in the Savannah Ecological Zone

Abstract: Allocating clinical staff and pharmaceutical inventories to remote peripheral clinics remains challenged by sparse survey intervals. We implement an Empirical Best Linear Unbiased Predictor (EBLUP) small-area estimation model linking 2021 Population and Housing Census enumeration microdata with Ghana Demographic and Health Survey (GDHS) indicators.

August 2025
DOI: 10.5281/ics.2025.08

Community Health Planning and Services (CHPS) Compound Retention Dynamics: Administrative Survey Evidence

Abstract: Community Health Officers (CHOs) are the foundational providers for Ghana’s primary healthcare delivery. Surveying 340 CHPS compounds across 6 regions, this study evaluates the structural determinants of midwife and community nurse tenure. We identify reliable rural solar electrification, clean borehole water, and designated staff housing as conferring an 8.4-fold increase in CHO 3-year retention.

Tags: Primary Care • CHPS • Nurse Retention • Solar Electrification • Maternal Health

Assessing Mobile Money Transaction Taxes on Informal Retailers: A Quasi-Experimental Panel Study

Abstract: Explores the behavioural shifts and cash substitution patterns among 1,840 informal market women and micro-retailers in Greater Accra and Ashanti regions following digital levy adjustments. We document a 24% temporary dip in electronic wallet transaction velocity immediately following policy implementation.

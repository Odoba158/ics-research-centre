Ghana District Socio-Economic & Fiscal Disparities Panel Dataset (2016–2024)

Version 2.1 (Annual)

Harmonized longitudinal panel indicators across all 261 districts in Ghana. Integrates local IGF revenue collections, DACF allocations, public primary school pupil-teacher ratios, access to potable water, and feeder road network density.

Geographic Coverage:
Ghana (261 Districts, 16 Regions)
Temporal Span:
2016 – 2024 (Annual frequency)
Available Formats:
CSV, Stata (.dta), Parquet, GeoPackage
Access Rights:
Open Access (CC-BY 4.0 with attribution)
Identifier: DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.ics.data.2025.01

High-Frequency Staple Food Price Surveillance Microdata (2024–2026)

Bi-Weekly Updates

Continuous point-of-sale retail and wholesale price series covering 24 staple commodities across 42 commercial market nodes in Ghana (e.g. Makola, Agbogbloshie, Kejetia, Techiman, Tamale Central). Measures inter-market arbitrage margins and seasonal volatility.

Geographic Coverage:
42 Key Market Centres in Ghana
Temporal Span:
Jan 2024 – Present (Bi-weekly)
Available Formats:
CSV, JSON API, SQLite
Access Rights:
Open for academic & policy use
Identifier: DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.ics.data.2026.02
